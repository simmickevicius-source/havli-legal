Privacy Policy

How Havli collects, uses, and protects your data.

Effective 19 September 2026

Havli is a household budgeting app. This policy explains what information it collects, why, who it’s shared with, how long it’s kept, and the rights you have over it.

Who is responsible for your data

Havli is developed and operated by Simonas Mickevičius, trading as an individual (individuali veikla) in Lithuania. For the purposes of data protection law, Simonas Mickevičius is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: sim.mickevicius@gmail.com

What data Havli collects

Havli is used by a household (a small group of people, such as a family or housemates, sharing one budget). Creating an account and using the app involves the following personal data:

  • Account data — the email address and password you sign up with, handled by our authentication provider (see “Who we share data with” below). We never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Household & membership data — your household’s name, currency, a display name and colour you pick for yourself, and your role within the household.
  • Financial figures you enter — expenses, income entries, savings goals and contributions toward them, each with an amount, category, date, and an optional note, all typed in by you or another household member.
  • To‑dos — task titles, who they’re assigned to, due dates, notes, and an optional cost.
  • Calendar events — event titles, times, location, and which household member they’re associated with.
  • Push notification data — a device token used to deliver notifications (like bill reminders or a weekly digest) to your phone, and your per‑member notification preferences.
Havli does not connect to your bank or any financial institution. There is no bank-linking, no Open Banking integration, and no third-party financial data source anywhere in the app. Every expense, income, and savings figure is a plain number you (or someone in your household) type in yourself.

Why we collect it

All of the data above is collected for one reason: to run the core functionality of the app — letting your household track shared expenses, income, savings goals, to‑dos, and a shared calendar, and to notify you about the things you’ve asked to be notified about. Household financial data is visible to the other members of your household by design, since that shared visibility is the point of the app. It is never used for advertising, and Havli has no analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind built in.

Who we share data with

Havli uses a small number of third-party service providers to operate the app. They act as data processors on our behalf, under their own data processing terms — we don’t sell or share your data with anyone beyond what’s needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — provides the database, authentication, and backend hosting that store and serve all of the data described above. Our Supabase database is hosted in the EU (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland), so this data stays within the EU/EEA — there’s no international transfer involved for this processor.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications to your device. Google receives your device’s push token for this purpose only. As part of Google’s global infrastructure, this does involve processing outside the EU; Google provides Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as its standard safeguard for that transfer.

Havli does not currently have any payment or subscription billing provider integrated, so no billing or payment data is collected or shared. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

How long we keep your data

Active household data (expenses, to‑dos, calendar events, and contributions toward savings goals) is kept for as long as your household uses Havli. To keep old, no-longer-relevant entries from piling up indefinitely, a scheduled housekeeping job automatically archives and removes entries once they are more than 24 months old (based on their original date, such as the date an expense occurred or a to‑do was created).

When an entry is archived this way, a snapshot of it is kept separately for a further 6 months before being permanently deleted, so that a mistaken automatic removal could still be investigated or recovered from in that window. Put simply: no individual expense, to‑do, or calendar entry is kept for longer than about 30 months after the date it originally relates to, and the system enforces this automatically on a monthly schedule.

Account data (your email address) and your household’s own current data are kept for as long as your account exists.

Your rights

If you’re in the EU/EEA or UK, data protection law (GDPR) gives you rights over your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data — most of this you can already edit directly in the app.
  • Delete your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and to data portability where applicable.

Havli doesn’t yet have a self-service “delete my account” button in the app itself. To exercise any of these rights — including requesting a copy of your data or having your account and its data deleted — email sim.mickevicius@gmail.com and we will action it directly.

Children’s privacy

Havli is intended for adults managing a household budget. The app does not currently ask for or verify anyone’s age during sign-up, so we have no reliable way to detect if a child has created an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we don’t design any feature specifically for them. If you believe a child has used Havli and provided personal data, please contact us at sim.mickevicius@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

Security

Household data is protected by database-level access rules that only allow members of a household to read or write that household’s own data. Push notification device tokens are restricted further, visible only to the account they belong to. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. If we make a material change — for example, adding a new third-party service that processes your data — we’ll let you know inside the app. The “Effective” date at the top of this page always reflects the version currently in force.
